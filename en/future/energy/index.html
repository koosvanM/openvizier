★ FUTURE · ENERGY

How we make energy

Three questions under one subject: how energy is generated and distributed, how the evolution could go, and what the architecture should look like on a European scale. Five articles distributed across those three questions.

★ I · HOW IT HAS DEVELOPED

What is already there in terms of technology, and what can be done with the existing infrastructure.

  • What is already there

    Overview of technical projects: SeaSkin, GuardSkin, AeroSkin, SolarSkin, TerraClean, Tyre patents, conical vortex reactor.

★ II · HOW THE EVOLUTION COULD GO

A fundamentally different route — not with magnetic cages, but with fluid dynamics as the foundation.

  • A fusion reactor without magnetic cage

    An alternative reactor design: the conical vortex reactor. Plasma is not contained with magnets but accelerated in a rotating flow.

★ III · HOW IT SHOULD WORK

Concrete execution on regional and European scale — what Brussels actually gets.

  • The biomimetic power plant

    Carbon-Alert R&D whitepaper: the optimal system for energy processing from biomass over 25, 50 and 100 years. Three horizons — 2050, 2075, 2125.

  • The Rapeseed Multiplier

    Brandenburg and Mecklenburg-Western Pomerania build on roughly 285,000 hectares of winter rapeseed today. With the Carbon-Alert architecture, a multiple of yield is achievable.

  • What Brussels actually gets

    Carbon-Alert at European scale: six million hectares of rapeseed, twenty million tonnes of soy meal imports, and the full CAP budget.
