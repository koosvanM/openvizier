What this is exactly about

The Open Visor is a free information paper without advertising. Whoever signs up here is requesting information — nothing more and nothing less.

  • No opinion you agree with. Signing up is not an endorsement of any position.
  • No sale of data. Your e-mail address is never passed on to third parties.
  • No advertising. You only receive announcements of new pieces in your chosen category.
  • Frequency: at most a few notices per month, often less.
  • Unsubscribe with one click. Every notice contains a direct unsubscribe link at the bottom.

This editorial team complies with the European GDPR. Your e-mail address is only used to send the requested notices.
