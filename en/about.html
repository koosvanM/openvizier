About this newspaper

What The Open Visor wants, what it does not want, and how it works.

What it wants

To make visible the connections that do not appear in specialist media. Between physics and political science, between education and economics, between ship coatings and democracy. In plain language, for anyone who wants to think along without first spending ten years in study.

What it does not want

No party politics. No advertising. No tracking. No sale of reader data. No clickbait, no outrage for the sake of outrage. And above all: no blinkers.

How it works

Each month an edition appears with a handful of articles grouped around a single thread. Below every article there is a discussion section where readers may respond by name. AI assists with translation, spam filtering and weekly summaries — editorial decisions remain human.

How it is funded

The newspaper has neither an advertising model nor a subscription model. I bear the costs myself. Readers who wish to contribute can do so via a one-off or annual donation — more on that in a later edition. For now: reading and participating is free and will remain free.

Who writes

Jacobus van Merksteijn, Dutch-speaking, based between Switzerland, Malta and Mallorca. Founder and developer of GuardSkin Ltd (coatings), Carbon Alert / TerraClean (biomass and CO₂ storage), and SolarSkin (solar-heat-resistant coatings). Working on a conical vortex fusion reactor and a 7-dimensional physics model.

My languages are Dutch, English and German. My trade is seeing connections. This newspaper is my attempt to share that with others.
