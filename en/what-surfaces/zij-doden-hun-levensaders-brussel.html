Malta, 19 June 2026 · Diagnosis

They are killing their lifeblood

Brussels and The Hague have an autoimmune disease. The patient is called the Netherlands.

Jacobus van Merksteijn · 19 June 2026

No rhetoric. Three reports from this week.

15 June 2026

Dutch stables are being dismantled and rebuilt in Poland, Sweden, Croatia, Peru, and Ukraine. It is precisely the young stables that are leaving. They are the most valuable.

NieuwRechts, 15 June 2026

16 June 2026

ESB calculates: the nitrogen buyout policy costs €212.795 per kilo. A kilo of nitrogen in compound feed costs €1,50. The policy is 140.000 times more expensive than the problem.

ESB, 16 June 2026

12 June 2026

Rabobank: agricultural added value –20% in one year. Fertilizer +26%. Energy +17%. Those who stay pay more for less.

Nieuwe Oogst / Rabobank, 12 June 2026

One week. Three sectors of the same victim.

And the farmer is not alone. DGA: business succession taxed up to 70%. Box 3: tax on profits that do not exist. Industry: CBAM is driving away Tata, Nyrstar, and the chemical industry. Craftsman: regulatory pressure and VAT are breaking the mkb sector. Brussels: approves more buyouts, blocks lab-grown meat, pushes through CBAM.

One leadership. Six sectors. One pattern.

The citizen has one lifeblood: production. That is where the money comes from for healthcare, education, defense, AOW. No production, no money, no society. A simple calculation.

That artery is now being severed by the leadership that lives off it.

And for what? For goals from 2019, in a climate of 2026. The plants have already moved. The tree line is shifting northward. Nature moves along. The leadership does not. They buy out farmers based on calculation models that reality has already outpaced. That is not stubbornness. That is the clinical picture.

Why does nobody think?

How can this be? Educated people. Advisors. Models. And yet, this.

True thinking requires three things.

Observing what is. The diseased leadership only sees its own models.

Doubting one's own assumptions. Within the diseased leadership, doubt is treason.

Taking responsibility. Within the diseased leadership, responsibility lies nowhere — it came from the model, it was in the directive, it was a European mandate.

Those who do not observe, do not doubt. Those who do not doubt, do not decide. Those who do not decide, do not think.

This is why calls for "better policy" do not work. A non-thinking system does not think better just because you ask it to.

The disease has a name

In medicine, we know this. Autoimmune disease. The body attacks its own organs. Pancreas, joints, myelin, thyroid, intestines.

The attacking cells are not malignant. They are doing their job. The problem lies in recognition. One's own tissue is mistaken for an enemy.

The immune system cannot see its own error. Every attempt at correction is treated as a threat and neutralized.

Read this description again. Replace "body" with "the Netherlands". Replace "immune system" with "Brussels and The Hague". It fits word for word.

This is not a metaphor.

This is a clinical picture.

Untreated, it leads to death — in humans within years, in a country within a decade.

Why a new minister solves nothing

The cell line is diseased, not the individual cell.

A new minister comes from the same university, the same career path, the same models, the same committees. He cannot do otherwise than make the same decisions. The ministry produces them, not him.

In medicine, this was learned long ago: broad immunosuppression — suppressing the symptoms — makes the patient weak and dependent. Healing requires something else.

Removing the cell line and forming a new one.

Five ascending treatment routes — medical and political side by side.

The treatment

Five medical routes. Five political translations.

1 · Suppressing symptoms

Subsidies, allowances, purchasing power packages. Prolongs the disease. Makes the patient dependent on the attacker. Do not do it.

2 · Removing specific institutes

Just as CAR-T kills the faulty B-cell line. Concretely, this week:

  • Lbv-buyout: stop.
  • Box 3 fiction: gone.
  • CBAM: withdraw.
  • BOR-tightening: roll back.
  • AERIUS: start over, different premises.

Five decisions. One hundred days. Non-negotiable.

3 · Full reset

Constitution, electoral system, abolishing ministries. Works — or destroys. Only if routes 2 and 4 are refused. Held in reserve.

4 · New organs alongside the old ones

Not reforming the FNV — founding a productive workers' movement alongside it. Not persuading the CPB — establishing a Bureau for Productive Future that does factor in BiCRS. Not pluralizing the NOS — citizen foundations for media. Not adapting the Eerste Kamer — a Bondsraad in which farmers, mkb, craftsmen, and inventors hold seats.

Building up next to tearing down. The old becomes irrelevant of its own accord.

5 · The nutritional base

Own energy through BiCRS. Own food. Productive currency. Wealth for employees and the mkb. Without this, nothing will last.

The prescription

  • Route 2 + 4 + 5. Combination. In that order. Fast.
  • Route 1 deliberately phasing out. Those who make the patient dependent feed the disease.
  • Route 3 in reserve. Only if the patient actively refuses 2 and 4.

From diagnosis to goal — four steps, no more.

What if we do nothing

The course of the disease is documented. Other countries have already gone through it.

Productive people leave. Basic organs fail. Healthcare waiting lists are growing. Defense cannot pay for itself. Capital follows the farmers to Poland.

Ten years. Then recovery is no longer possible.

To you

The leadership is not going to heal itself. An autoimmune system does not recognize its own error.

You do.

You are the farmer whose stable is disappearing to Poland. The DGA whose succession has been made unaffordable. The mkb owner whose bill is exploding. The employee whose wage is shrinking.

You are also the healthy counterforce. The Treg cell that can support the recovery. But only if you rouse yourself.

Acknowledge the disease. Name it. Demand treatment. Do not wait for the patient.

They are killing their lifeblood.

Whoever reads this sentence and thinks "it won't be that bad" — they have already succumbed to the disease.

Whoever reads and knows what to do — they are a Treg in the making.

The recovery begins with you.
