France — revolutionary moment

Four classical conditions fulfilled simultaneously. Plus a generation that legitimises violence, plus a physical aggression infrastructure that is already running.

Jacobus van Merksteijn

Sources
  • Ifop / Fondation Jean-Jaurès / MAIF (juni 2026) — La passion militante
  • Ifop / Club Landoy (juni 2026) — enquête pensioenen
  • Odoxa / Franceinfo / Le Figaro (juli 2026)
  • Ipsos-baromètre (juli 2026)
  • Le Monde · Fondapol · LCP Assemblée nationale · Challenges · Arab News / AFP · Aplutsoc · Le Journal Toulousain

France is not on its way to a revolutionary moment. France is already inside it — only the trigger and the form remain to be determined.

1. What the polls say

Four independent French polls of June–July 2026 simultaneously reach their outermost quantile. That is no longer a “warning signal” — it is the condition itself.

  • Violence legitimacy among youth. 54% of 18–24-year-old activists believe one should sometimes use violence to advance ideas. Among activists over 65, only 12%. Ifop / Jean-Jaurès / MAIF.
  • System trust. 72% no longer trust the system for a decent pension; 87% among 18–34-year-olds. Ifop / Club Landoy.
  • Social wave expectation. 8 in 10 French expect another yellow-vest-style movement if fuel prices rise further. Odoxa.
  • Presidential defiance. Macron at 74% unfavourable — historic low just before the 2027 election. Ipsos baromètre.

2. The mobilisation infrastructure is ready

“Bloquons tout” on 10 September 2025 was leaderless, digitally organised, without union backing — and yet forced 80,000 police on the streets. For the autumn 2026 return this pattern is repeated, now with union support.

  • Bloquons Tout Île-de-France has been organising since July 2026 with the explicit ambition to “worry power as in September 2025”. Aplutsoc.
  • 18 September 2026: intersyndicale mobilisation day; up to 900,000 expected; 80,000 police planned. Le Journal Toulousain.
  • 1 May 2026 delivered — according to organisers — over 1 million marchers in more than 200 cities.

3. Four classical conditions — all present

Historical sociologists (Skocpol, Tilly, Goldstone) identify four simultaneous conditions that define a revolutionary situation. All four are now present in France.

Condition France 2026
Fiscal state crisis Fitch and S&P downgrades; the 2026 budget only survives through PS censure threats.
Paralysed political system Three blocks since June 2024; three prime ministers in one year; Lecornu II barely survives the censure of 6 July 2026 (132 in favour, 289 needed).
Elite divisions Zucman tax demanded by the left flank and rejected by Senate + Assembly; Piketty vs Macron; De Courson commission criticises the tax authority itself.
Mobilised mass with violence legitimacy 54% youth pro-violence; Bloquons tout 2.0; intersyndicale 18 September.

The cooling of the fiscal route is not a counter-force — it is displacement of method.

4. Where the aggression lands in policy

  • Speculative funds commission (Assembly, unanimous 16 June 2026): ban on majority takeovers of strategic firms, blacklist of tax havens, mandatory beneficial-owner transparency.
  • Highest fortunes commission (De Courson report, 8 July 2026): eleven recommendations, explicit finding that the tax authority cannot grasp the real wealth of the very rich.
  • LFI rhetoric summer 2026: Panot (revoke nationality of exiting rich), Mélenchon (“46,000 ultra-rich pay no euro tax”), Piketty (referendum on a € 800 bn solidarity tax).

5. The apparent cooling is displacement

Odoxa July 2026: support for “tax the rich harder to close the deficit” falls from 80% (2024) to 68% (2026), sharpest among 18–34s (73–78% → 55–57%). Headline of Challenges/Odoxa: “la haine des riches s'essouffle en France”.

Wrong reading: hatred cools down. Correct reading: the same age group that abandons the fiscal route is the one where 54% now consider violence legitimate. The 12 points lost on tax-as-a-solution do not disappear into resignation. They shift into other methods.

6. Likely scenarios (July 2026 – 2027)

Scenario Form Trigger
1 · September 2026 as first release Bloquons tout 2.0 + intersyndicale 18/9 + budget debate + next downgrade. Budget 2026, fuel price, rating downgrade
2 · Physical aggression spreads beyond crypto H2 2026 – H1 2027. First high-profile case outside crypto makes the pattern public. First incident outside crypto
3 · Election crisis 2027 RN wins or comes very close. Elites refuse legitimation. Post-electoral escalation possible. 2027 outcome, court rulings
4 · Targeted intimidation of individuals In addition to 1 or 3. Industrialists, former ministers, prominent entrepreneurs as “symbols of the rich”. Symbolic figure caught in media storm

Conclusion

The usual scientific caution — “correlation is not prediction” — no longer applies once four or five independent indicators simultaneously, in the same direction, reach their outermost quantile.

What the sources do not determine is the timing and the precise form: classical revolution, permanent disruption à la yellow vests, or violent polarisation without takeover. But that the base conditions for a revolutionary moment are fulfilled is, on the data of June–July 2026, no longer a cautious claim.
