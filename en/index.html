Foreword from the publisher

Why this newspaper?

People often ask how one person can do so many different things at once — designing coatings, sketching a fusion reactor, writing about democracy. My answer is always the same: because it is all the same thing.

Whoever understands the flow of water around a ship's hull also understands why a society stagnates. In this newspaper I make those connections visible.
