What you need to do now

Within a few minutes you will receive a confirmation email from The Open Visor at the address you provided. Open that email and click the confirmation button inside. Only then are you officially subscribed.

This is a legal requirement (GDPR) and protects you from unsolicited mailings.

No email received?

Wait 5–10 minutes — delivery can sometimes be delayed. Check your spam or promotions folder; the very first email sometimes ends up there. Verify that you entered the correct email address. If it still does not work: redactie@openvizier.org.

What to expect next

Every month, on Saturday morning, you will receive a new edition of The Open Visor. No advertising, no tracking. Unsubscribe with one click at the bottom of every email.
