Why the Climate Edition?

Because the climate debate in Europe has been going in circles for years. Subsidies against subsidies. Policy against policy. Prohibitions against orders. What the discussion never does: honestly lay out the whole field, without artificially keeping the visible price of political preference low.

The Climate Edition does. Every page uses the same basis of calculation: no SDE++, no ETS credit, no RED bonus, no vehicle tax exemption, no feed-in tariff. Only what stands technologically and economically on its own. Plus the human side: who builds, who pays, who benefits.

Explore all chapters above. Read above all the central triptych at the top. And ask yourself one thing after reading: will Europe sit on the box — or on the luggage rack?
