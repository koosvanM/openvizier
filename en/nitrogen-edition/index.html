★ Nitrogen edition

Three dossiers, one animal, one answer

The Dutch nitrogen crisis has been treated for years as three separate problems — nitrogen, methane, soy dependency — by three ministries and three committees. A fourth, forgotten winner is waiting: the farmer in the transition zone. Here you will find the concrete answer to the question that policy leaves open.

Lead article · June 2026

Three problems, one answer

Four crisis points — nitrogen, methane, soy, farm income — solved by one production architecture: frost-resistant Juncao with pressure explosion. One hundred thousand hectares in transition area delivers up to 27 per cent of Dutch agricultural emissions as climate effect. With preservation of grassland and a factor ten to fifteen higher farm income.

For politicians and policymakers: this is not a lobby against nitrogen policy. This is the concrete answer to the question that policy leaves open.

For farmers: this is a fourth option alongside quitting, arable farming or extensifying. A cash crop with preservation of grassland and a factor ten higher income.

For dairy buyers: this is your EUDR and scope-3 route. Local protein production, validated under Dutch conditions, scalable to a substantial share of soy imports.
