AI assistance

The Open Visor uses AI for:

  • Translation of articles into German and English (with human review).
  • Initial filtering of reader responses for spam and insults.
  • Weekly summaries of the sharpest counter-arguments.
  • Research support for facts and sources.

Editorial decisions — what we publish, what we write, how we frame it — remain human. The publisher bears final responsibility.

Privacy

The Open Visor collects as little data as possible:

  • No cookies except those technically necessary (for example for the discussion widget and language preference).
  • No tracking — no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no ad networks.
  • Newsletter subscription: only your e-mail address is stored, with double opt-in. Cancellable at any time.
  • Comments: handled via GitHub authentication (Giscus). Your data remains with GitHub under their privacy policy; we receive only your username and the text of your comment.
  • No sale, no sharing of data with third parties, except where legally required.

Corrections policy

Errors are not quietly edited away but corrected visibly, with the date and nature of the correction noted beneath the article.
