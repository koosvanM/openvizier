The Open Visor uses AI for translation (DeepL), initial filtering of comments for spam and abuse, weekly summaries, and research support. Editorial decisions — what to publish, what to write, how to position it — remain human. The publisher bears final responsibility.

Privacy

No cookies except technically necessary ones. No tracking — no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel. No sale or sharing of data with third parties, except where required by law.

Corrections policy

Errors are not quietly deleted but visibly corrected, with the date and nature of the correction noted below the article.
