Denk aan de natuur — in negen minuten

De kernboodschap van het integrale klimaatvoorstel in beeld en gesproken tekst.

Nine minutes, one argument. The Netherlands has the knowledge, industry, ports and trading position to become Europe’s first climate-negative modern economy within twenty years. Four proven pathways that together form a closed carbon cycle, with permanent underground plastic storage as the foundation.

What it contains

  • Why CCS alone is not enough, and why an additional pathway is needed that truly removes CO₂ from the atmosphere
  • The four pathways: tropical co-cultivation, biobased plastic that stores CO₂, cement and timber replacement, and polymer aerogel insulation
  • Underground plastic storage as the foundation: why putting plastic in the ground is precisely the opposite of what our instincts tell us
  • The fiscal aspect: from €334 million in annual penalty charges to €433 million in carbon certificates
  • The six concrete steps the government can take now
  • What it delivers: 51 to 70 megatonnes of CO₂ removal per year, €6 billion in avoided oil imports, and 100,000+ jobs in partner countries

Who it is for

For the Dutch Government, the House of Representatives, the European Commission, and anyone who wants to understand why the Netherlands can write a different climate story from what is currently on the table. Nine minutes is enough time for the calculation and the conclusion. For the full substantiation, there is the longer article.
