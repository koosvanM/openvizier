The Kosaris — Book Three — Education

The book of Anthea — a filmic essay in three parts

Part I — The Fourteen Precepts · 12 minutes

In which the book of Kosus is read in Europe — and Klytia hears her own words again. Anthea begins. The fourteen precepts are prepared.

Part II — The Four Pillars Lived · 20 minutes

In which the four pillars are no longer taught — but in upbringing become breathing daily habits.

Part III — The Second Line · 12 minutes

In which the distinction is no longer a doctrine but a second line: what is done alongside what is said.

About this film

Three films. Three moments in the life of Anthea, keeper of the fourteen precepts. What is education when you must pass on an old teaching to people who never knew its source? What remains when the last witness departs?

Part I opens with the fourteen precepts — the original text of Kosus himself. Part II shows how Anthea passes on the four pillars not as doctrine but as daily habit. Part III is the second line: the teaching that happens without words, in the action beside the action.
