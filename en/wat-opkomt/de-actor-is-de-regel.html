The actor is the rule

Why reform is impossible and replacement inevitable — and how the medicine of autoimmune disease shows us how the Dutch system can truly be healed.

Jacobus van Merksteijn · Malta, June 2026

A tetralogy in four parts

  1. Room for primal feeling — about what is present in the child before it can speak, and how education and technology quietly drive it out.
  2. The feeding line — about the leader without self-knowledge, the courage that is lacking, and how the system reflexively expels its rebels.
  3. The immune system that attacks healthy cells — about the autoimmune response of organisations and political systems.
  4. The actor is the rule — this piece. The fourth layer: why no system therapy is sufficient as long as the actor itself is the system.

Four articles, one line of reasoning — here brought to the foundation of a revolution.

In three earlier contributions, we constructed a line of reasoning that begins with the newborn child and ends with the diseased system. In this fourth piece, that reasoning is taken to its ultimate consequence. What emerges is no longer a diagnosis and no longer a call for better policy. What emerges is a revolutionary question: which actors will still exist in 2030, and which will have been replaced?

The three earlier layers, briefly summarised

Layer 1 — In Room for primal feeling we established that something is present in human beings before they can speak: a direct, bodily attunement between people. No mysticism — a neurophysiological fact, supported by Porges and McGilchrist. But this primal feeling is unconsciously unlearned by an upbringing and education system wholly directed towards what is measurable, verbal and cognitive. What does not fit into a test is made invisible. What is made invisible dies out. This is the first expulsion — not of people, but of a capacity within people.

Layer 2 — In The feeding line we followed what happens to that expelled human being in adult life. “The courage to say that something is wrong is lacking through a lack of self-knowledge — a structural error, not a character flaw.” Our institutions, procedures, decision-making and education are built on evolutionary action and thought: small steps, consensus-building, caution. Those who operate within them are accepted. Those who operate outside them are by definition alien to the system. Today’s education prepares people for yesterday. We do not learn what the future needs.

Layer 3 — In The immune system that attacks healthy cells we described the consequence at system level. A healthy body recognises invaders and eliminates them. A diseased body attacks its own healthy cells. That is how the Netherlands now operates: the farmer, the SME, the owner-director, the skilled craftsperson, the inventor, the knowledgeable rebel — all healthy cells attacked by autoantibodies. Not out of malice. Out of self-preservation by the diseased system.

Layer 4 — the actor itself

And now the step that turns the three earlier layers into a revolutionary conclusion: it is not the structures that are diseased, but the actors. The Ministry of Climate is not a neutral apparatus implementing misguided policy. It is a collection of people shaped by education, selection and culture in such a way that they no longer know primal feeling, have not dared to develop self-knowledge, and therefore automatically act as autoantibodies against any divergent knowledge. The CPB is not coincidentally blind to BiCRS — the CPB is the organisation that can calculate only what fits the old model. The FNV does not produce anti-productive policy by accident — the FNV is the crystallised expression of a worldview that sees productivity as a threat.

The error is not that our institutions act wrongly.
The error is that our institutions are what they are.

Reform is impossible because the reformer and the object to be reformed are one and the same. The only path is replacement — and that begins by recognising that the current actors will never themselves pass judgement on their right to exist.

This is not rhetoric. This is precisely what we have had to learn in medicine when treating autoimmune diseases. For generations, doctors tried to “re-educate” the diseased immune system, “adjust” it, “calm it with immunosuppression”. After fifty years, what became clear was this: the autoimmune B-cell line is not a healthy cell that has been wrongly calibrated. It is a cell line whose identity itself is autoimmune. You cannot re-educate it. You must remove it. After that, new B cells can arise from naïve stem cells — cells that never had the faulty programming.

This is what CAR-T-cell therapy does for lupus, multiple sclerosis and scleroderma. And this is what politics in the Netherlands needs.

Five medical therapies, five political equivalents

The medicine of autoimmune disease knows five increasingly advanced treatment routes. Each has a direct political counterpart. Set side by side, they immediately show where Dutch parties and administrators stand — and what the necessary end goal is.

Five ascending treatment routes — from symptom suppression (route 1) to microbiome restoration (route 5). The winning combination: routes 2 + 4 + 5.

Route 1 · Broad immunosuppression ↔ subsidies and purchasing-power packages

The doctor administers prednisone. The immune system is suppressed as a whole — healthy and diseased alike. The symptoms recede for a while, but the disease continues and the patient becomes dependent on the treatment. Side effects accumulate, infections threaten, and the body weakens.

Translated into politics: subsidies, allowances, energy compensation, purchasing-power packages, generic wage increases through collective agreements. This is the mode of GroenLinks-PvdA, D66, VVD, CDA and NSC. It is the mode of FNV and CNV. Everyone receives something, the patient feels briefly relieved, but the underlying disease process — the system’s attack on its own productive tissue — continues unabated. And every payment makes the paying institution more indispensable.

Route 2 · CAR-T-cell therapy ↔ dismantling specific institutions

The doctor harvests T cells from the patient and programmes them with a receptor (CD19 or BCMA) that specifically recognises and kills the faulty B-cell line. The rest of the immune system remains intact. In lupus, MS and scleroderma, patients have entered drug-free remission after a single treatment.

Translated into politics: this is not “a smaller government”; it is not “tax relief in general terms”. It means concretely abolishing the Box 3 deemed-return system. Withdrawing CBAM. Removing the nitrogen-assessment circuit (committee, modellers, lawyers) as a whole. Dismantling the climate-modelling apparatus. JA21 and BBB point cautiously in this direction; parts of FvD and PVV do so more sharply. But pointing is not the same as implementing. CAR-T works only when the markers are precise — otherwise healthy cells are hit as well.

Route 3 · Autologous stem-cell transplantation ↔ institutional revolution

The doctor first harvests the stem cells, then chemically wipes out the entire immune system, and infuses the patient’s own stem cells back in. It is a reset: a fresh immune system is built from naïve cells, without the autoimmune memory. It works — or destroys. The interim phase is vulnerable: in immune aplasia, any infection can be fatal.

Translated into politics: institutional revolution. Constitutional revision, electoral-system reform, abolishing entire ministries, monetary restructuring, revising the EU position. Nova Democratia/VMP and parts of FvD point in this direction. The method works — but requires strong logistics and political immunity to panic during the interim phase. Unsuitable for mild cases; for terminally refractory situations, sometimes the only option.

Route 4 · Treg therapy and tolerogenic cells ↔ new actors alongside the old

This is the most overlooked and at the same time the most fundamental route. The doctor cultivates regulatory T cells (Tregs) in the laboratory, multiplies them, programmes them with a receptor for the specific autoantigen, and infuses them back. Or: the doctor reprogrammes dendritic cells so that they present the autoantigen with a peace signal. The immune system relearns that this tissue belongs to the self. Not brute suppression, no total reset — re-education through a new cell line alongside the existing one.

Translated into politics: do not “reform” the FNV, but establish a productive workers’ movement after the FNV. Do not “pluralise” the NOS, but build a citizen-funded pluralistic media network that makes the NOS irrelevant. Do not “adjust” the Senate, but establish a Council of the Federation that structurally embeds a counterweight of knowledge. Do not hope that the university will reform itself, but establish free academies that make the old ones redundant. This is not adaptation; it is replacement — but in a fluid form, with construction preceding demolition.

Route 5 · Nutrition and the microbiome ↔ the economic-cultural microbiome

The least spectacular and perhaps most fundamental route. Short-chain fatty acids — formed by gut bacteria from fibre — directly regulate Treg formation. A poor microbiome produces too few Tregs, resulting in chronic inflammation. A disrupted intestinal wall constantly lets stimuli through that inflame the immune system. Without restoring the microbiome, no other therapy works permanently.

Translated into politics: this is the economic-cultural microbiome of the Netherlands. BiCRS/ethanol as an endogenous carbon cycle. Food sovereignty. A productive currency that finances innovation instead of recycling debt. Wealth formation among employees and SMEs rather than among asset funds alone. Education for the future instead of the past. And, in a line that leads directly back to the first manifesto of this tetralogy: room for primal feeling in primary school, before the cognitive edifice is erected. That is where Treg formation literally begins: in an upbringing that does not first expel the child.

The revolutionary insight

No single route works on its own. The winning combination is routes 2 + 4 + 5: targeted dismantling of pathogenic institutions, the parallel construction of new actors alongside the old, and restoration of the economic and cultural nutritional base.

Route 3 — a complete institutional reset — remains in reserve for when routes 2–4–5 are made impossible by the patient’s own immune blockade. That is: if the diseased patient refuses treatment and chooses further symptom suppressants.

Route 1 — subsidies and purchasing-power packages — must be deliberately phased out. Not abruptly, because the patient has become dependent. But every extension of route 1 increases the power of the attacking cell line.

The attacking actors and the protected tissue

Anyone who wants to stop the autoimmune response must first explicitly name which actors are attacking and which tissue is being attacked. In current Dutch politics, that distinction has been deliberately blurred — linguistically, legally and morally — in order to continue the attack without resistance. Here is the clear designation:

The rebel · naming and course correction Consensus-media, CPB monoculture, party discipline Citizen media foundations, Council of the Federation, Nova Democratia/VMP

The revolution roadmap

The combination of insights from the tetralogy and the medical analogy produces a concrete, followable route. Not revolution as a storm that sweeps everything away, nor revolution as a violent moment — but revolution as cell replacement in a living organism, in four successive phases.

From diagnosis to goal in four steps — CAR-T intervention (first 100 days), forming stem cells (1–3 years), new organs alongside the old, feeding the microbiome for the long term.

Step 1 — The CAR-T intervention · first 100 days

Targeted. Fast. Specific. No broad sweep, no “we are going to change everything”, but three or four sharply defined interventions whose markers are unambiguous. Scrap the Box 3 deemed-return system and replace it with taxation of actual returns on capital. Withdraw CBAM. Dismantle the nitrogen-assessment circuit. A handful of other bottlenecks as candidates. Do not abolish entire ministries — it is too early for that, and the next system must first be ready — but remove specific cell lines, as CAR-T does.

Step 2 — Stem-cell formation · construction phase 1 to 3 years

Naïve tissue is formed in parallel with the CAR-T intervention. This is where Nova Democratia/VMP differs in principle from an ordinary “new party in the existing landscape”. A naïve stem cell cultivated in the contaminated thymus acquires the same error — therefore the cell line must be formed outside the existing system. Its own member culture, its own selection criteria based not on dossier knowledge but on self-knowledge (in the sense of layer 2: courage, self-reflection, recognition of primal feeling). Its own training for administrators, free from the frameworks of thought that caused the contamination. This is work measured in years, not months — but it is the most important work of the entire revolution.

Step 3 — Establish new organs · parallel structures

Council of the Federation alongside the Senate. Free academies alongside the universities. Citizen media foundations alongside the NOS and major newspapers. Productive workers’ associations alongside FNV and CNV. Co-ownership funds alongside the pension-fund monoculture. Not within the old — alongside it. The old will naturally become irrelevant once the new organs function — electorally, economically, culturally. That is the tolerance route: not fighting with antibodies, but forming new Tregs that recalibrate the system.

Step 4 — Feed the microbiome · the long term

Build the BiCRS/ethanol carbon cycle. Anchor food sovereignty. Introduce a productive currency or preserve the euro from fiscal expropriation. Broaden wealth formation among employees. And, fundamentally, revise education so that primal feeling is not first expelled before the cognitive edifice is erected. Treg formation begins in primary school. Anyone who fails to raise a free, productive generation may lose their institutions to a new autoimmune cycle again by 2050.

The medical warning

Step 1 without steps 2–3–4 = reckless surgery. You remove pathogenic tissue without having a replacement cell line ready, and the body reacts with overcompensation or collapses.

Steps 2–3–4 without step 1 = parallel structures neutralised by the pathogenic cell line before they can function. The autoantibodies then remain free to attack every new Treg population.

Only the combination works. In the right order. With respect for the organism’s pace.

What this means politically in concrete terms

For the Dutch voter, the practical question for every party that emerges in the coming years is: at what level does this party operate? A party that sells only route 1 (purchasing-power packages, broad subsidies) prolongs the disease. A party that promises only route 2 (scrapping regulations) without offering route 4 performs surgery without replacement tissue. A party that promises route 3 (a total reset) without supporting route 5 risks death from malnutrition during the interim phase. Only parties that offer routes 2 + 4 + 5 in conjunction are operating at a revolutionary level in the medical — that is, effective — sense of the word.

For the current institutions, the message is this: a ministry cannot reform itself. A trade union cannot do so by itself. A media organisation cannot do so from its editorial board. Not because the people within them are bad — they are often intelligent and well-intentioned — but because their reason for existing, their selection mechanism, their career ladder and their internal language together constitute the rule that programmes them as an autoantibody. Anyone who tries to establish a productive workers’ movement within the FNV will be expelled or neutralised by the FNV. Anyone who tries to introduce pluralism within the NOS will be thwarted by its own editorial culture. The cell line does not replace itself.

The one sentence of the tetralogy

The four articles together can be summarised in one statement. We propose that this sentence become the motto of the movement carrying this revolution:

The motto of the tetralogy

What is expelled from the child as primal feeling becomes visible in the adult as a lack of self-knowledge, and in institutions as an autoimmune reflex against every healthy cell. The actors themselves are the rule — and therefore only replacement, not reform, can save the organism.

This is not a mantra to frame and hang on a wall. It is a diagnosis, a roadmap and a programme of work. Anyone who truly understands this sentence knows what must happen over the next five years — and which actors must still exist in 2030 and which must not.

In conclusion · the invitation

The revolution of Het Open Vizier is not a revolution of streets and stones. It is a revolution of cell replacement. Patient, targeted, fundamental — and irreversible once the new cell lines are functioning. It is precisely the revolution a living organism needs to recover from an autoimmune disease: four or five parallel treatment routes, deployed at a followable pace, carried by people who were raised outside the contaminated thymus.

Medicine teaches us that this is possible. Lupus patients who were suppressed with prednisone for years enter drug-free remission after a single CAR-T treatment. Scleroderma patients who had no reflex treatment left regain their lives after a stem-cell reset. Type 1 diabetics whose islets of Langerhans had been eaten away preserve their remaining islets under Treg therapy. No miracle, no mysticism — cell replacement at the right level, in the right order, with respect for the microbiome.

What is possible in the body is possible in a political system. Provided we have the courage to truly face the diagnosis. Provided we have the courage to say that something is wrong — the courage lacking through a lack of self-knowledge, as layer 2 already taught us. Provided we are willing not to try to convince the existing actors, but to build new ones alongside them. Provided we no longer allow primal feeling to be expelled from our children, because that is tomorrow’s source of Tregs.

The tetralogy is therefore complete. A diagnosis in four layers. A treatment in five routes. A roadmap in four phases. And one motto holding everything together.

The rest is up to us — up to you, reader — to carry forward. Not by persuading the old institutions to change their minds. But by patiently and deliberately helping the new ones come into being.

The question to you, reader

Three questions that this tetralogy passes on to you:

  1. Which new actor would you like to help bring into being? A free academy in your field. A citizen media foundation in your region. A productive workers’ movement in your sector. A co-ownership fund in your company. Cell-line replacement begins with those who volunteer to be naïve stem cells.
  2. Which old actor will you no longer feed? With your membership fees, your clicks, your vote, your attention, your participation in procedures that you know no longer work. The pathogenic cell line feeds on your continued participation.
  3. Which layer are you yourself? Do you still feel the expulsion of primal feeling (layer 1)? Do you recognise the lack of self-knowledge in yourself or in your leaders (layer 2)? Have you witnessed the autoimmune response against a healthy idea (layer 3)? Or have you yourself by now become an actor in a new cell line (layer 4)? Your answer determines what you must do now.

Responses via the newsletter will be read — and, where they add value, included in a follow-up. This is not a monologue. This is an organism seeking to learn to recognise itself anew.
