EDITIE 8 — AUGUSTUS 2026

The Supertanker

Europe is a vessel. The captain stands aft, the prow crumples at the fore. Not out of malice — but from a limitation of vision inherent to every vast apparatus. This edition observes with attentiveness, reverence, and the gaze of a child that which truly stands, and proposes: a second wheelhouse, not as a replacement, but as an aid for those who sail on board.

Augustus 2026 · By Jacobus van Merksteijn · Malta

Eén voorwoord en dertien afleveringen, elke week één. Of in deze editie alles tegelijk — vier delen die op elkaar voortbouwen, voorafgegaan door het geopolitieke kader. Lees ze in volgorde, of pak het deel dat u trekt. Elke aflevering staat op zichzelf.

Foreword

A Vessel Whose Arrival We Long to Witness

Europe as a supertanker, with the captain positioned at the stern and a bow already buckling beyond his field of vision. A perspective from Malta upon the vessel on which we all sail.

·

A Vessel We Wish to See Arrive

Europe is a vessel. The captain strives, yet the vessel is too vast to be surveyed in a single glance — the creaking prow remains beyond his field of vision. This edition gazes from Malta with intent, with reverence for those labouring on board, and with the eyes of a child who wishes first to perceive what is truly present.
