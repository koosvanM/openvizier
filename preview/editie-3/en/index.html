What edition 3 is

Edition 3 of Open Vizier is not a collection of separate pieces. It is a single coherent body of thought, built from articles, reference works, images and charts.

It describes a psychological and pedagogical model: the primal feeling as foundation, the three brain layers it rests on, and what we systematically squeeze out of children in modern education and parenting.

The tone is direct, honest, undiplomatic. Those who want to attack are welcome — that comes with Open Vizier.
