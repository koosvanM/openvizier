Nova Democratia — The Answer

The sixth edition posed the question: how might Europe regain its status as a global player? The seventh edition provides the answer via two physical levers that operate exclusively in tandem — and only under a different administration. Giant Juncao and BiCRS (a product of Carbon-Alert Ltd) provide the burgeoning carbon axis. Plastic derived from household waste in German open-cast mines provides the storage axis.
