The Window is Closing — Why Three to Five Years

Since 2010, Chinese consortia have promoted Giant Juncao across sixteen provinces in eight countries. The implications of Europe missing the window for catching up, and the geopolitical scenarios that would subsequently remain.

By Jacobus van Merksteijn · Malta
PDF Word

What this instalment covers

- Chinese presence in Giant Juncao exports: active promotion campaigns since 2010 in Papua New Guinea, Fiji, the Central African Republic, Zimbabwe, Rwanda, and Madagascar — more than twelve thousand farms in sixteen provinces across eight countries. - What a geographical head start entails: local breeding stations, local product lines, local knowledge bases, local trade networks — achievements that cannot be overtaken by European capital injection alone. - The parallel with solar panels: Europe was the inventor, China became the producer, and Europe became the consumer. Without swift intervention, this pattern shall repeat itself for BiCRS and biofuel derived from Juncao. - The parallel with semiconductors: ASML demonstrated what European industry can still deliver when a single company is supported by a single government for thirty years. However, one company does not constitute a continent. - The Russian chain reaction: as soon as Russia loses its fossil energy, its economic model collapses. The question remains who will provide the replacement energy. He who possesses the Juncao axis, possesses the answers. - The American alternative: Trump's cabinet of lawyers is not building BiCRS facilities. That is an invitation that is not forthcoming.

This instalment is currently being further developed with a timeline graph of Chinese Juncao exports since 2010 and the three geopolitical scenarios in the event of missing the window.
