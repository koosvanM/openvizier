The two levers combined — a technical roadmap

A brief technical roadmap of what follows in this edition. The production axis and storage axis as a single industrial design, with the core figures that sustain each subsequent chapter.

By Jacobus van Merksteijn · Malta
PDF Word

This opening establishes the logic that the subsequent six instalments elaborate upon technically. Two levers, one objective, one administrative prerequisite.

The figures underpinning this edition

- Giant Juncao: 300–450 tonnes of green biomass per hectare per year routinely, with a record of 853 tonnes in Papua New Guinea — three to ten times more productive than sugar cane or switchgrass. - Plastic in household waste: 78% carbon on average, 70% of waste incineration CO₂ emissions — 35 megatonnes/year in Europe, directly avoidable. - German open-cast mine volume: Garzweiler (~2 billion m³) + Hambach (~2.5 billion m³) + Inden + Welzow-Süd = hundreds of years of European plastic stream storage. - Time window: 3–5 years before Chinese geographical dominance in Giant Juncao fields becomes irreversible. - Result of synergy: Europe from +3.4 GtCO₂/year (current) to net zero within ten years, subsequently net negative.

This instalment is currently being further developed with the complete technical roadmap and the full diagram of both levers.
