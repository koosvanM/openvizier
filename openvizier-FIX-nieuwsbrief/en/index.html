Publisher's foreword

Why this newspaper?

People often ask me how one person can do so many different things at once — designing coatings, sketching a fusion reactor, writing about democracy. My answer is always the same: because it is all the same thing. Whoever understands the flow of water around a ship's hull also understands why a society stagnates.
