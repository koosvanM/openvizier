### EN
**The anti-immune disease of Brussels: three simultaneous attacks on the European economy**

Since 1 January 2026, Brussels runs three mechanisms in parallel:

🔴 **CBAM** — €75/tonne CO₂ on industrial imports. €2.1 bn per quarter in costs. NO rebate for European exporters to third markets.

🔴 **ETS expansion** — free allocation declines from 97.5% to 0% by 2034. ETS2 for buildings and transport in 2027.

🔴 **Pillar Two** — 15% minimum tax dismantles all national fiscal incentives for industry and innovation.

And the working European carbon cycle? **BiCRS / bio-ethanol via Carbon-Alert**: €40/tonne, €22.4 bn per year gross for Congo, €8,000 per hectare for the farmer. Ignored by RED III in favour of hydrogen for 2040.
